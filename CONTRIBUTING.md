# Contributions to HomeWaf

Original HomeWaf work is source-available under the root noncommercial
license. Do not submit third-party material without its applicable license,
copyright notices, provenance and redistribution rights. Changes to existing
third-party files must preserve their licenses and modification notices.

Before merging a contribution that will be commercially relicensed, obtain
and retain an explicit written contributor agreement from its rights holder.
The agreement must authorize the publisher to use, reproduce, modify,
distribute, sublicense and commercially relicense that contribution, and
identify any employer or third-party rights. A pull request, DCO sign-off,
silence or this document alone is not proof of such permission. Existing
upstream contributions remain under their existing licenses.

Run the targeted regression checks relevant to the change and the build.
Do not commit private signing keys, customer data or production credentials.
